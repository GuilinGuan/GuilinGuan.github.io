个人简介
======
我目前为大连理工大学软件工程博士在读（预计2030毕业），导师为[樊鑫教授](https://faculty.dlut.edu.cn/Xin_Fan/zh_CN/index.htm)。2026年获沈阳航空航天大学计算机科学与技术专业硕士学位，导师为[周唯教授](https://yjs.sau.edu.cn/info/1027/1164.htm)。2025年，于中国科学院宁波材料技术与工程研究所智能医学影像团队开展科研工作，导师为[赵一天教授](https://ytianzhao.github.io/)。我的研究兴趣主要涵盖**计算机视觉与医学图像分析**。具体研究方向包括基础模型在医学图像分析中的应用、智能体赋能的医学图像分析、少样本与跨域少样本学习，以及世界模型在医学图像分析中的探索与应用。目前已在 **IEEE JBHI、BSPC、IEEE SPL、BIBM、ICONIP** 等国际期刊和会议发表或录用多篇论文。同时，担任 **Acta Materialia、Array、BSPC、EAAI、ESWA、IEEE SPL、IEEE Access、Neural Networks、Neurocomputing、MBEC** 等国际期刊的审稿人。<strong><em style="color: #d32f2f;">诚邀对相关研究方向感兴趣的本科生、研究生交流合作，共同探索前沿研究问题！</em></strong> 

About Me
======
I am currently a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at Dalian University of Technology, with an expected graduation in 2030, under the supervision of [Prof. Xin Fan](https://faculty.dlut.edu.cn/Xin_Fan/zh_CN/index.htm). I received my M.S. degree in Computer Science and Technology from Shenyang Aerospace University in 2026, under the supervision of [Prof. Wei Zhou](https://yjs.sau.edu.cn/info/1027/1164.htm). In 2025, I conducted research with the Intelligent Medical Imaging Group at the Ningbo Institute of Materials Technology and Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of [Prof. Yitian Zhao](https://ytianzhao.github.io/). My research interests lie primarily in computer vision and medical image analysis. My current research focuses on the application of foundation models to medical image analysis, agent-empowered medical image analysis, few-shot and cross-domain few-shot learning, and the exploration of world models for medical image analysis. I have published or had papers accepted in international journals and conferences, including IEEE JBHI, BSPC, IEEE SPL, BIBM, and ICONIP. I also serve as a reviewer for several international journals, including Acta Materialia, Array, BSPC, EAAI, ESWA, IEEE SPL, IEEE Access, Neural Networks, Neurocomputing, and MBEC. <strong><em style="color: #d32f2f;">I warmly welcome undergraduate and graduate students interested in these research areas to connect and collaborate on exploring cutting-edge research topics!</em></strong>
